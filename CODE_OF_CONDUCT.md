# Code of conduct

Iles is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be
respectful, assume good intent while discussing ideas, give actionable feedback,
and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained
disruption are not accepted in project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
unsafe or repeatedly disruptive.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile
or private security-reporting channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical, with attention to the safety of the person reporting.
